01 — AI BASICS

What is Artificial Intelligence?

AI is a broad field focused on building systems that can perform tasks associated with human intelligence, such as recognising patterns, learning from data, understanding language and supporting decisions.

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
The broad field
MACHINE LEARNING
Systems learn patterns from data
DEEP LEARNING
Neural networks with multiple layers

🤖 Narrow AI

Designed for specific tasks such as recommendations, image recognition or language assistance.

🧠 General AI

A hypothetical concept of AI with broad human-like intelligence across many tasks.

⚡ Generative AI

AI systems that can generate new text, images, audio, software code and other content.

04 — THE OTHER SIDE OF AI

Deepfakes, Privacy & Trust

Powerful technology can also be misused. Responsible AI requires prevention, detection, transparency and accountability.

🎭 Deepfakes

Manipulated or generated media can impersonate people and spread misinformation or fraud.

🔒 Privacy

AI systems may process personal data, making data minimisation, security and lawful use essential.

⚖️ Bias

Biased or unrepresentative data can produce unfair outcomes.

🔍 Transparency

People should understand when AI affects important decisions and know how to seek human review.
